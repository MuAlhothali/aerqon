# AERQON Founder Outreach Pack

This document supports early founder-led outreach for AERQON.

AERQON is currently a local synthetic prototype. This outreach pack must not be used to imply production readiness, live AWS connectivity, customer adoption, revenue, compliance certification, or proven risk reduction.

## Current boundaries

- DEMO DATA
- SYNTHETIC ENVIRONMENT
- NOT CUSTOMER DATA
- NO LIVE AWS
- No production readiness claim
- No commercial validation claim
- No authentication
- No billing
- No database
- No AI/LLM
- No automatic remediation
- No compliance certification
- No guaranteed risk reduction

## Outreach goal

The goal is not to sell immediately.

The goal is to find whether small SaaS teams have a real, repeated, painful problem around proving that cloud security remediation was actually completed.

## Target people

Prioritize people who understand cloud operations, security findings, audits, customer security reviews, or remediation follow-up.

Good targets:

- Technical founders
- CTOs
- DevOps engineers
- Cloud engineers
- Security engineers
- GRC or compliance owners
- Managed security service providers serving SaaS teams

## Target company profile

Best fit for early discovery:

- Small or medium SaaS company
- Uses AWS or AWS-related tooling
- Has customer security reviews, audits, or compliance pressure
- Has limited security team capacity
- Tracks cloud findings through tools, tickets, spreadsheets, or manual follow-up

Poor fit for early discovery:

- No cloud usage
- No security review pressure
- No remediation tracking
- Wants only a generic vulnerability scanner
- Requires production integration before any discussion

## Positioning statement

AERQON explores a simple problem:

Cloud teams can often detect misconfigurations, but proving that remediation was actually completed is harder than marking a ticket as resolved.

AERQON demonstrates an evidence-to-verification workflow where a finding is resolved only after fresh compatible evidence supports a passing evaluation.

## One-sentence pitch

AERQON is a synthetic prototype for cloud security remediation assurance: it helps demonstrate how teams can separate detection from verified resolution.

## Call opening script

Thank you for taking the time.

I am not here to pitch a finished product. AERQON is currently a synthetic prototype.

I am trying to understand your current process after a cloud security finding is detected:

- Who owns the fix?
- Who verifies it?
- What evidence is required?
- What happens when something is marked resolved too early?

## Discovery questions

1. What tools do you currently use to detect cloud misconfigurations?
2. What happens after a finding is assigned for remediation?
3. Who decides that the finding is resolved?
4. What evidence is required before it is considered resolved?
5. Do you preserve the original evidence and the fresh recheck evidence separately?
6. Have you seen findings marked resolved before they were truly fixed?
7. What happens if a finding is falsely marked as resolved?
8. Do audits, customer reviews, or internal reviews require proof of remediation?
9. How long does it take to prepare that proof?
10. Would an evidence package after recheck be useful?
11. Which integrations would matter most first?
12. What would make this idea not useful for your team?

## Qualification scoring

| Area | Weak signal | Medium signal | Strong signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem frequency | Rare | Occasional | Recurring |
| Pain level | Low | Medium | High |
| Evidence pressure | None | Internal only | Audit/customer-driven |
| Ownership clarity | No clear owner | Shared owner | Clear accountable owner |
| Willingness to test | No interest | Review demo | Wants pilot/sample report |

## Strong validation signals

- They describe a recent painful remediation verification example.
- They mention audit or customer security review pressure.
- They distrust manual resolved labels.
- They need baseline and fresh evidence preserved separately.
- They ask for a sample report.
- They ask how a pilot would work.
- They would introduce you to a cloud/security owner.

## Weak validation signals

- They do not track remediation evidence.
- They trust scanner status labels fully.
- They do not have audits, reviews, or customers asking for proof.
- No one owns verification.
- The problem is rare.
- They only want a generic scanner.

## What not to say

Do not say:

- AERQON is production-ready.
- AERQON connects to live AWS.
- AERQON has customers.
- AERQON reduces risk by a proven percentage.
- AERQON provides compliance certification.
- AERQON performs automatic remediation.
- AERQON uses AI to make security decisions.
- AERQON replaces CSPM/CNAPP tools.

## What to say instead

Use accurate wording:

- AERQON is a local synthetic prototype.
- AERQON explores evidence-based remediation verification.
- AERQON demonstrates the principle that detection is not verification.
- I am validating whether this problem is painful enough to justify building live integrations.

## Minimum outreach target

Before building live AWS integration, aim for:

- 30 targeted outreach messages
- 10 customer discovery interviews
- 3 strong pain examples
- 2 pilot or paid assessment signals
- 1 clearly ranked first integration path

## Founder decision rule

Do not build live AWS integration only because it is technically interesting.

Build it only after discovery shows that teams repeatedly struggle to prove remediation with trustworthy evidence and are willing to spend time or money solving it.
